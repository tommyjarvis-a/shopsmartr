                Choose an app icon

                Changes right away in the app and on the browser tab. The icon on your home screen stays the same: iPhone keeps the icon from when the app was added, and only changes it if you add the app again.

                Import recipes from any site

                Send a recipe page straight to the app. It's read on your phone; nothing goes through a server.

                Bookmark (Safari, or any browser)

                1. Tap Copy bookmark code.
                2. In Safari, bookmark any page (Share › Add Bookmark) and name it Happy Wife.
                3. Open Bookmarks, tap Edit, open the new bookmark and replace its address with the copied code.
                4. On a recipe page, tap the address bar, then the Happy Wife bookmark.

                On a computer, drag this to your bookmarks bar: Happy Wife

                iPhone Shortcut

                1. Add the Happy Wife Import shortcut from the link Steven sends.
                2. On a recipe page in Safari, tap Share › Happy Wife Import.
                3. It opens the recipe here and copies it. In the home-screen app, go to Recipes › Import a recipe and tap Paste.

                The home-screen app keeps its own recipes, separate from Safari. A recipe opened in Safari can be copied over with Copy for the home-screen app.
